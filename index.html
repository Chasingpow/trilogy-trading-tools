Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED · SEPTEMBER 3, 2026

This policy explains what Trilogy Trading Tools ("we", "us") collects when you use our products — the 3T Pro Scanner, Halal 3T Scanner, Trilogy Journal, and Wealth Builder — why we collect it, and the control you have over it. We keep the data we hold to the minimum needed to run the products.

What we collect

  • Account information — your email address, and a display name if you set one.
  • Data you enter — trades, setups, notes, goals, budgets, and preferences inside the apps.
  • Ticker lookups — the stock symbols you search, used only to fetch market data.
  • Technical data — basic logs and cookies strictly needed to keep you signed in and secure.

What we deliberately do NOT collect or store

  • Payment card details — checkout and billing are handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Brokerage passwords — any brokerage link is handled by a dedicated secure provider. We never receive your broker login.
  • Government IDs, SSN/SIN, or bank account numbers.

Service providers

We use a small set of trusted vendors, each bound to protect your data: Supabase (database & authentication), Stripe (payments, PCI-DSS certified), Railway (hosting), and our market-data providers for the tickers you look up.

How your data is protected

  • Encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest.
  • Row-level security in the database — your records are technically walled off so no other user can read them.
  • Access to production systems is limited and authenticated.

Your rights

You can access and export everything we hold about you, correct it, or permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. Depending on where you live you may have additional rights under PIPEDA (Canada), CCPA/CPRA (California), or GDPR (EU/UK) — we honour these requests for all users. To exercise any right, use the contact section above.

Retention, cookies & children

We keep your data while your account is active; deleting your account removes your personal data (limited billing records may be retained by Stripe for tax purposes). We use only essential sign-in cookies — no advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Our products are not intended for anyone under 18.

This is general information, not legal advice, and does not itself create a contract.
