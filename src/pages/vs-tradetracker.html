Pick TradeTracker if

  • You want the lowest price: one plan at $29 USD/mo, with a free week to try it.
  • Your edge work starts from broker-imported trades and backtested strategies.
  • You already have a scanner and charts you like.

Pick Trilogy if

  • You want a live gapper board with dilution, squeeze and exit levels on every mover, next to the journal that grades the trade.
  • You want trading profits to land in a budget instead of evaporating back into the market.
  • You want a Sharia-screened universe, prices in CAD, and a 7-day refund.

FAQ

Is Trilogy cheaper than TradeTracker?

No. TradeTracker is one plan at $29 USD/mo. The 3T Pro Scanner alone is $175 CAD/mo and the Trader Pack (scanner + journal) is $225 CAD/mo. The difference buys a live gapper board with dilution, squeeze and exit levels, plus a budgeting tool TradeTracker does not have.

Does Trilogy have a free trial?

Not a free trial. Every monthly tool comes with a 7-day full refund if it does not earn its keep, and you can cancel anytime after that.

Is Trilogy's data real-time?

Yes. The scanner runs on real-time US stock data from Massive (formerly Polygon.io), refreshing about every 8 seconds from premarket through after-hours.
