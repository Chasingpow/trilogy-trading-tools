Pick TradeZella if

FAQ

Is the Trilogy Journal cheaper than TradeZella?

No. The Trilogy Journal is $100 CAD/mo; TradeZella starts at $35 USD/mo. Trilogy is built to sit next to the 3T Pro Scanner, and the Trader Pack puts both together for $350 CAD/mo.

Can I use the Trilogy Journal without the scanner?

Yes. Every tool rents on its own, month to month, and you can cancel anytime.

Can I try Trilogy before committing?

Every monthly tool comes with a 7-day full refund if it does not earn its keep.
