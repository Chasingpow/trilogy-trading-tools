FAQ

Is Trilogy cheaper than Trade Ideas?

Month to month it is about the same: the 3T Pro Scanner is $175 CAD/mo and Trade Ideas Standard is $127 USD/mo, roughly $175 CAD at recent exchange rates. On annual billing Trade Ideas is cheaper ($1,068 USD/yr against $1,890 CAD/yr). Trilogy adds a board tuned for small-cap momentum and, in the bundles, a journal and a budget app that Trade Ideas does not include.

Does Trilogy include real-time data?

Yes. Real-time market data is included in the scanner price, with no separate data feed to add on.

Can I try Trilogy before committing?

Every monthly tool comes with a 7-day full refund if it does not earn its keep, and you can cancel anytime after that.
