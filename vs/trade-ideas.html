FAQ

Is Trilogy cheaper than Trade Ideas?

No. The 3T Pro Scanner is $300 CAD/mo against Trade Ideas Standard at $127 USD/mo. The price buys a board tuned for small-cap momentum plus, in the bundles, a journal and a budget app that Trade Ideas does not include.

Does Trilogy include real-time data?

Yes. Real-time market data is included in the scanner price, with no separate data feed to add on.

Can I try Trilogy before committing?

Every monthly tool comes with a 7-day full refund if it does not earn its keep, and you can cancel anytime after that.
